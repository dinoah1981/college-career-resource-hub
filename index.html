A Little Bit About Me

I hold a BFA from Temple University's Boyer College of Music and Dance, where I made the Dean's List.

Before Comp Sci High, I taught dance to students in grades K to 5, worked in nonprofit administration, fundraising, and board relations, served with AmeriCorps through Public Allies in Chicago, and wrote about the cultural arts.

Today I work one-on-one with seniors planning for college, students already enrolled, and recent graduates starting their careers. Together we tackle financial aid, career readiness, and the day-to-day stuff that comes with building a life you are proud of.

My goal is simple: help every CSH alum reach financial independence and well-being.

Outside of work, you'll find me bumping a volleyball in Astoria Park, roaming the city in search of the best bookstore and coffee combination, and running my own professional dance collective, Graceful Edge Dance.
